Within 2 business days (San Jose, California — Pacific Time).

What to include

To help us help you faster, please include:

  • The product and version you're using (e.g. PrivateClaw iOS 0.0.1+22, macOS 0.0.1+22)
  • The device and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro on iOS 18.4, MacBook Pro on macOS 15.3)
  • A short description of what you were trying to do and what happened instead
  • Any error messages — screenshots are welcome

TestFlight beta feedback

If you're testing PrivateClaw via TestFlight, the in-app "Send Beta Feedback" option (long-press the app icon or shake the device) routes your report to us with diagnostic information attached. That's the preferred channel for beta bug reports.

Privacy and data

PrivateClaw is designed so that your API keys, prompts, and conversation data stay on infrastructure you control. We don't have access to that data and can't recover it on your behalf. See our Privacy Policy for details on what we do and don't collect.

Business inquiries
