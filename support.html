Tunnelless

Tunnelless joins a Tailscale network from inside the app itself — there is no VPN profile to install and no separate Tailscale app to download.

  • Signing in. Press Connect. The app opens Tailscale's sign-in page in a sheet and returns you to the app automatically once the device joins. You need a Tailscale account; signing in happens on Tailscale's own page, not ours.
  • Trying it without an account. Press Demo mode to see the interface with sample data, with no sign-in and no network access.
  • If sign-in does not open. The app shows the reason on screen. Press Connect again, and if it persists, email us that message — it names the cause.
  • Starting over. Sign out & reset clears the local device registration. To remove the device from your tailnet, and to delete a Tailscale account, use the Tailscale admin console — that data lives with Tailscale, not with us.

TestFlight beta feedback

If you're testing an Indiagram app via TestFlight, the in-app "Send Beta Feedback" option (long-press the app icon or shake the device) routes your report to us with diagnostic information attached. That's the preferred channel for beta bug reports.

Privacy and data

PrivateClaw is designed so that your API keys, prompts, and conversation data stay on infrastructure you control. We don't have access to that data and can't recover it on your behalf.

Tunnelless connects your device to your own Tailscale network. Your account, your devices and your traffic are between you and Tailscale — we operate no server in that path and hold none of it.

See our Privacy Policy for details on what we do and don't collect.
